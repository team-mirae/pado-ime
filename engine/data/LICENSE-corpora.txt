Dictionary headwords (known words, counted at least 3)
- mecab-ko-dic. Apache-2.0. open-korean-text. Apache-2.0.

en-words.txt is JIS Moonshot's common-en.txt (team-mirae/jis-moonshot, MIT); its generated part is
derived from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org, CC BY 2.0 FR) English sentences, counts only.

ja-words.txt is JIS Moonshot's common-ja.txt (team-mirae/jis-moonshot, MIT): hand-written everyday
Japanese words in kana.

hanja.tsv is JIS Korean's hanja dictionary (team-mirae/jis-korean): see LICENSE-libhangul-hanja.txt
and LICENSE-unicode.txt.

kanji.bin is built from Mozc's open-source dictionary: see LICENSE-mozc.txt.

ja-kana-native.txt (Japanese words usually written in kana; built into the engine) holds counts only,
from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org, CC BY 2.0 FR) Japanese sentences, read with SudachiPy and
SudachiDict core (Apache-2.0), by tools/build_kana_native.py. So does ja-common-words.txt (the most
common Japanese words not written in kana; built into the engine): words and readings with counts.

en-words.txt's `#! rare` section is Webster's Second New International Dictionary (1934), in the
public domain, as macOS ships it in /usr/share/dict/words (engine/tools/build_en_rare.py).

key-models.bin (the key models of the layout-set ranker; built into the engine) holds key n-gram
statistics only, no text, built by tools/build_keymodel.py and examples/keymodel.rs from: ko-words.tsv
(the sources above); Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org, CC BY 2.0 FR) English and Japanese sentences, the
Japanese read with SudachiPy and SudachiDict core (Apache-2.0); the Leipzig Corpora Collection's
English sentences (CC BY; Goldhahn, Eckart and Quasthoff, LREC 2012); and statutes: 17 U.S.C.
(govinfo.gov, public domain), 日本国憲法 and 民法 (e-Gov 法令API; 著作権法第13条), 대한민국헌법 and 민법
(ko.wikisource.org; 저작권법 제7조).
