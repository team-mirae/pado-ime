pado ime 0.3.1: bada ime 0.3.1 on Windows, and the first installer.

**Install:** download `pado-ime-0.3.1-setup.exe` below and run it (UAC). Then pick **pado ime** with
Win+Space (under 日本語), and restart apps that were running. Windows 10 or 11, x64. Uninstall from
Settings → Apps; your settings and dictionary stay.

From bada 0.3.0 and 0.3.1:

- **bada plain (16)**, the new default henkan layout: vowels under the left hand, consonants under the
  right. Settings already saved keep their layouts. [docs/layouts/16-bada-plain.md](https://github.com/team-mirae/pado-ime/blob/main/docs/layouts/16-bada-plain.md)
- **Key models** rank the layouts of a set: with several layouts enabled, the first candidate is right
  more often (93.9%, from 82.3%).
- **A word and its particle**: with QWERTY in a set, `Claude에`, `report를`, `GitHubで` in one run.
- **A picked row is fixed by typing on**, and its layout sticks.
- **⌃変換 fixes what shows** (bada's ⌃かな): it stays, staged, and the keys after it start anew.
